These are the scripts in the KupCAD repository's
[`examples/`](https://github.com/kupcad/kupcad/tree/main/examples) folder. Each is a complete,
parametric part; open one in the playground, move its sliders, and read the script to see how
it is made. The guides build two of them, the [mug](/guides/parts/solids/) and the
[gearbox](/guides/parts/gears/), from the first line, and
[Mastering KupCAD](/mastering/introduction/) builds six more step by step.
